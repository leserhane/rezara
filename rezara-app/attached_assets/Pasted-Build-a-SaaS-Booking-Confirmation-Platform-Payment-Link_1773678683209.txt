Build a SaaS Booking Confirmation Platform (Payment Link Based)

You are a senior full-stack engineer building a production-ready SaaS platform.

Create a modern web application that allows businesses (restaurants, salons, clinics, consultants, service providers) to confirm reservations using a payment link.

The system works like Stripe payment links but for booking confirmations.

The business defines the reservation, not the customer.

The customer only receives a confirmation link and pays a deposit.

Core Product Concept

Businesses often receive booking requests via:

• Phone
• WhatsApp
• Instagram DM

After discussing with the customer, the business decides the reservation details.

The platform allows the business to:

Create a reservation manually

Generate a unique payment confirmation link

Send that link to the customer

Customer opens the link and pays the deposit

Booking becomes confirmed automatically

The customer cannot choose the appointment time.

All booking details are predefined by the business.

Example Use Case

Customer messages restaurant:

"Table for 4 on Saturday?"

Restaurant checks availability.

Restaurant creates reservation in dashboard:

Customer: Khalil
Date: March 30
Time: 20:00
Guests: 4
Deposit: 50 MAD

Platform generates link:

reza.com/reservation/k30034

Restaurant sends link via WhatsApp.

Customer opens page and pays deposit.

Reservation becomes confirmed.
